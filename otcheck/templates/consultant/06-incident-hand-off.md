Use this when the review suggests a live compromise. Call the client's contact first, and send this note afterwards. You're handing over to the client's incident process, and the consultant doesn't start the response alone.

**Date and time:** [time]
**Called:** [name, phone, time]
**Site:** [site]

[Facts only: the system, the address, the evidence and where it came from.]

## Why it matters
[What could happen to the process, safety or food.]

## Suggested first steps, for the operator to approve
- Confirm that the process is safe, and use manual control if needed.
- Agree how to isolate the system without losing monitoring or alarms.
- Preserve logs and record settings before changing anything.
- Check for changed setpoints, disabled alarms, unknown accounts and changed passwords.
- Involve the vendor.
- Report to the national cyber authority or sector regulator, as the client's obligations require.
