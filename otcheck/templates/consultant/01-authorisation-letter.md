# Authorisation letter

> Template only. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review it before use.

**Reference:** [reference]
**Date:** [date]

[Client legal name] ("the Client") authorises [consultant or company] ("the Consultant") to carry out the review described below.

**Authorised by:** [name, job title]. I confirm I have authority to give this permission for the systems listed.

**Valid from** [start date] **to** [end date].

## Sites and addresses in scope

| Site | Public IP addresses | Owner of the address, if not the Client |
|---|---|---|
| [site] | [addresses] | [provider or contractor] |

Where a contractor or provider owns a system, the Client confirms it has their written permission.

## Permitted work

- Looking up the listed addresses in third-party internet scan data
- Reviewing documents and configuration exports supplied by the Client
- Interviewing staff and contractors
- Analysing network logs supplied by the Client

## Not permitted under this letter

- Scanning, probing or testing any system
- Attempting to log in, or testing passwords
- Changing any device, setting or account

Any of this needs a separate written agreement.
