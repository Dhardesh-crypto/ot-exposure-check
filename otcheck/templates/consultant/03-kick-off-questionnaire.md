# Kick-off questionnaire

**Client:** [name]  **Date:** [date]

## The business
- Which sites are in scope, and what does each one do?
- What would an hour, a day and a week of downtime cost at each site?
- What could harm people, food safety or the environment?
- Are you subject to NIS2, or to sector rules? Who owns that?

## People
- Who owns security for control systems: IT, engineering or a contractor?
- Who can approve changes to control systems?
- Who is on call out of hours?

## Systems
- What control systems run each site? Include make, model and firmware if known.
- Is there a network diagram and an asset list? How old are they?
- Which public IP addresses does each site use?
- Are any 4G or 5G routers fitted?

## Remote access
- Who connects remotely, and how?
- Which contractors have access?
- Is multifactor authentication required?

## Recovery
- When was manual operation last practised?
- When was a backup last restored?
- Has anything unusual happened recently, such as changed setpoints, alarms or accounts?
