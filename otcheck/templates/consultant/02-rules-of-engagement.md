# Rules of engagement

> Template only. Have a lawyer in your jurisdiction review it before use.

**Engagement:** [reference]

1. The work stays within the sites, addresses and dates in the authorisation letter.
2. The consultant doesn't connect to, scan or change client equipment.
3. Network captures are made by the client's staff or vendor, using a method agreed with the operator.
4. The client's operator has the final say on anything that could affect the process.
5. Findings are shared only with: [names or roles].
6. A suspected live compromise is reported by phone straight away to: [name, phone]. Don't wait for the report.
7. Evidence is stored on encrypted storage. Passwords are never collected. If one appears in a document, it's redacted and the client is told.
8. Reports are marked confidential and sent by: [agreed secure method].
9. Scope changes are agreed in writing first.
